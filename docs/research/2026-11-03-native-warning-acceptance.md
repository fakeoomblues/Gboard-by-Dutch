## Independent 100-announcement audit

The [raw-identity audit](../../tests/fixtures/navigation-warnings/warm-timing-verification.json) joins every original source ANNOUNCE `(provider_session, sequence)` to the receiver’s retained diagnostics and source dispositions. In the Maps replay cases, a real start arrived after cancellation had been requested. The receiving scheduler recorded it but returned before reporting STARTED to the source; the source then retained `INTERRUPTED, started=false` and granted the same authority again. Four new regression failures reproduced this together with unnecessary cancellation of identical speech on state/geometry updates. The shared fixes persist an actual start even during cancellation, reject replay after a started/interrupted/failed attempt, restrict retries to positive no-start outcomes, and retain an already accepted immutable payload when its audible content is unchanged. Changed unsaid speech still obtains a fresh grant after a confirmed stop and retains its original deadline. The full FM unit component passes after these changes; a new integrated source/consumer candidate and runtime rerun remain required.

Native speech review also found that incomplete synthesis or an error without a recorded start was being treated as proof of no output. Android distinguishes a confirmed pre-synthesis flush from interrupted synthesis; errors can occur at different stages. [Android’s callback contract](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/speech/tts/UtteranceProgressListener). Maps/Vela regression fixes now preserve uncertainty rather than authorizing speculative replay; their corrected native bindings and runtime checks remain in progress.

Genuine DriveDeck car-only testing reached Vela’s real CarSession and complete warning connection with no phone Activity, but exposed two remaining gaps: free-browse had not initialized the saved native voice engine, and the native speed-render callback was gated behind drive/navigation state. Temporary permissions, host preferences and process state were restored after both failed runs; these observations do not establish car warning acceptance.
